# What's new since v7.3.1

- **Play Zeptocore slices over USB MIDI.** Trigger slices from a keyboard or music app on channel 1. Releasing a note lets playback continue.
- **See your Zeptocore performance.** An optional browser, desktop, or plugin visualizer shows waveforms, slices, effects, and pad presses. Requires visualizer-enabled firmware and a copy of your samples.
- **Faster access within samples.** More efficient sample jumps, especially on scattered SD-card files. Preparation is automatic and remembered between sessions.
- **More room for effects.** Filters and tape delay run more efficiently while keeping their sound.
- **Fewer pitch-related freezes.** Fixes crashes during fast, pitched retriggers and at the highest MIDI pitch setting.
- **More reliable saved settings.** Existing saves still work; saved banks and samples restore more reliably.
- **Better sample handling.** Safer sample changes, better detection of alternate samples, and improved SD-card error recovery.
- **More reliable USB MIDI.** Better handling of notes, clock, and controls arriving together.
- **Better Ectocore/Ezeptocore clock restarts.** Resume on the intended beat, including after a manual stop. The low-latency version responds sooner.

Zeptocore's knob shortcuts have moved. Hold **A** or **B** while turning:

| Hold | Knob 1 (X) | Knob 2 (Y) | Knob 3 (Z) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Volume | Filter | Stretch |
| **B** | Random sequence | Pitch | Tempo |

See the [updated Zeptocore guide](zeptocore_v8.pdf) for the full controls.
